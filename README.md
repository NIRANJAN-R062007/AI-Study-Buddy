
📘 AI Study Buddy

AI Study Buddy is an intelligent learning assistant designed to help students study smarter, not harder. It supports learners by explaining concepts, answering questions, summarizing content, and improving overall productivity through AI-powered assistance.




🚀 Features

🤖 AI-powered doubt solving using Gemini AI

📚 Concept explanations in simple terms

📝 Notes summarization

❓ Question & answer support

⏱️ Productivity-focused learning assistance

🎯 Student-friendly and easy to use





🧠 Problem Statement

Students often struggle with:

Understanding complex concepts

Getting instant doubt clarification

Managing study time efficiently


AI Study Buddy solves this by acting as a personal 24/7 study companion.




💡 Solution Overview

AI Study Buddy leverages Gemini AI to:

Explain complex topics clearly

Assist with exam preparation

Support self-paced learning

Reduce dependency on multiple learning platforms

📱 Mobile application
